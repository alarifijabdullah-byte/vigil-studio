Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

In short: this site does not send your details to any server of ours or store them. What you type in the contact form becomes a message on your own device, and you choose whether to send it to us on WhatsApp.

Who we are

Vigil is a production, design, licensing and AI studio in Riyadh. Privacy questions: WhatsApp +966 50 144 4443.

Data you give us

  • Contact form: name, phone number, services and a note about your project. It stays in your browser and is turned into a message you open in WhatsApp or copy. It reaches us only if you send it.
  • WhatsApp conversations: we use what you send to reply, prepare a quote and do the work. We do not sell it or share it, except where your service requires it, such as preparing a licence file that you submit to the authority.

Data stored in your browser

A small flag in session storage keeps the intro from replaying during the same visit. It is cleared when you close the tab.

Third-party services

Your browser connects to these directly, so they receive ordinary technical data such as your IP address and browser type:

  • GitHub Pages: hosting.
  • Google Fonts: typefaces.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare): the 3D graphics library.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): when you tap a WhatsApp button.
  • Google Ads: if you arrive from an ad, we measure the visit and WhatsApp taps to learn which ads help. Google may use cookies for this; see how Google uses data in ads. You can turn off personalised ads in Ad Settings.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by messaging us on WhatsApp. We will respond within a reasonable time, in line with your rights under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law.

Retention

We keep client conversations and files for the length of the project and afterwards only as long as follow-up and invoicing need, then delete them on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of the page.
