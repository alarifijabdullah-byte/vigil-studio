    FAQ

    What’s the difference between the free and full service?

    The free version is a short one that shows you our work and where your business stands. The full service is the complete job: a full report, a full identity, your licence file, or launching the assistant. No commitment after the free one.

    Do you submit the hospitality licence for me?

    We prepare the full file and review it with you under the Private Hospitality Unit Regulation. You submit from your own account on the Ministry of Tourism platform, and we stay with you step by step. The decision is the Ministry’s, and government fees are paid to it.

    What does the location study include?

    We compare two to four neighbourhoods: competitors and their ratings, the area, rent and break-even. You get a PDF report with a clear recommendation.

    Do I own the logo and files?

    Yes. Once payment is complete, all files and design rights are yours.

    Do you work with clients in other cities or countries?

    Yes. We work remotely: message us on WhatsApp and we deliver the finished work.

    How does payment work?

    We send a fixed quote before we start. Half up front and half on delivery, by bank transfer or mada.
